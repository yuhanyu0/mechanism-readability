# Claim Boundary

## Supported

- Three-arm motifs exhibit a robust, transportable monotonic thickness-conditioned steering relation under the frozen proxy interface and held-out-object split.
- The single canonical fixed-boundary surface-minimization trajectory is not the best held-out predictor among the tested low-capacity laws.
- Ordinary centerline compression and plausible low/moderate measurement noise do not explain the empirical shallowness without additional failures.
- In the declared matched synthetic pair, equality of endpoints and the full low-order response trajectory does not imply equality of path-generating structure.
- Under the tested interface family, local path/tangent information is the first boundary-stable diagnostic layer with adequate measurement budget.

## Not supported

- The held-out object split is an independent external-dataset replication.
- The empirical generator is known.
- Surface optimization is generally falsified.
- The weighted or developmental rival is causally established in natural networks.
- The four-arm topology transition is validated as a transported empirical law.
- J3 is universally minimally sufficient.
- Boundary-condition averaging has been confirmed for the exact surface model.
- Static morphology establishes temporal memory or developmental history.

## Unresolved

- Exact noncanonical surface response family.
- Physical skeleton-radius to model-circumference mapping.
- Direct full-surface measurements in the empirical network families.
- Physical perturbations that modify thickness and track subsequent geometric response.
- Dynamic, final-state-equivalent matched rivals.

## Scope note

The empirical law and the matched synthetic benchmark are complementary evidence tracks. The synthetic benchmark calibrates what low-order observational equivalence can hide; it does not prove that the empirical networks were generated by either member of that matched pair.
